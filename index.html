I'm a passionate and self-driven front-end developer with a strong curiosity for beautiful interfaces, smooth interactions, and clean code. My journey into web development started with a desire to build things people can actually use — now it’s a daily obsession.

I believe that design is not just how something looks — it's how it feels and functions. I enjoy crafting responsive, user-friendly websites that blend aesthetics with performance. Whether it's writing semantic HTML, styling with modern CSS, or building logic with JavaScript, I love every part of the process.

Right now, I’m focused on improving my React skills, exploring the fundamentals of backend development, and building projects that challenge me to grow. In the future, I see myself as a full-stack developer working with teams that care about accessibility, performance, and elegant design.

Outside of coding, you can find me sketching ideas on paper, diving into UI inspiration, or reading about tech, business, and minimalism.
